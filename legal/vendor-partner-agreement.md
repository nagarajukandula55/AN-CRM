# AN-CRM — Vendor / Sub-Vendor Partner Agreement

**Last updated: 2026-07-28**

This Agreement supplements the AN-CRM Terms of Service and governs the
relationship between a Parent Vendor and its Sub-Vendors where the
Parent Vendor uses AN-CRM's sub-vendor hierarchy feature (Ultimate tier).

## 1. Nature of the Relationship

1.1 A Parent Vendor may onboard one or more Sub-Vendors under its AN-CRM
account, each operating as a distinct tenant for data-isolation purposes
but commercially and operationally subordinate to the Parent Vendor as
they arrange between themselves.

1.2 **AN-CRM is not a party to, and assumes no liability for, the
commercial relationship between a Parent Vendor and its Sub-Vendors**
(pricing they charge each other, service-level commitments, territory
arrangements, etc.). AN-CRM's only obligation is to provide the platform
functionality (account creation, data isolation, billing of the
sub-vendor addon charge) as described in the Terms of Service.

## 2. Onboarding and Payment

2.1 A Sub-Vendor account is created only after the Parent Vendor
completes payment of the applicable sub-vendor addon charge via
Razorpay, verified through AN-CRM's payment verification flow. This
charge is consumed on successful creation of that one Sub-Vendor account
(see the Refund & Cancellation Policy, Section 6) and must be repurchased
to add another Sub-Vendor.

2.2 The Parent Vendor is responsible for ensuring it has the legal right
and any necessary local licenses/registrations (e.g., GST registration if
applicable to that Sub-Vendor's turnover) to operate through a
Sub-Vendor in the manner it intends.

## 3. Data Isolation and Responsibility

3.1 Each Sub-Vendor's Customer Data is isolated from every other
Sub-Vendor and from unrelated vendors on the platform, per AN-CRM's
standard data-isolation architecture. The Parent Vendor may configure
visibility of a Sub-Vendor's data to itself where the platform's
role/permission model permits it.

3.2 The Parent Vendor is responsible for its Sub-Vendors' compliance with
the AN-CRM Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and applicable law
(including GST invoicing rules, consumer protection law, and data
protection law with respect to that Sub-Vendor's own end-customers). A
breach by a Sub-Vendor may be treated by AN-CRM as a breach by the Parent
Vendor for purposes of suspension/termination under the Terms of
Service.

## 4. Removal of a Sub-Vendor

The Parent Vendor may deactivate a Sub-Vendor account through the
platform at any time; no refund of the original sub-vendor addon charge
is issued on deactivation (see Refund & Cancellation Policy, Section 6).
AN-CRM may independently suspend a Sub-Vendor account for breach of the
Terms of Service regardless of the Parent Vendor's wishes, where required
for legal compliance or platform integrity.

## 5. No Employment or Agency Relationship

Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, agency, or
joint-venture relationship between AN-CRM and any Parent Vendor or
Sub-Vendor, or between a Parent Vendor and its Sub-Vendor (that
relationship, whatever its legal character, is a matter between those
two parties alone).

## 6. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by, and disputes resolved under, the same
governing law and dispute resolution provisions as the AN-CRM Terms of
Service (Section 12).
